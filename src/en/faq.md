## 1. How does ArcanaLogic know what each card means and create my interpretation?

ArcanaLogic uses its own interpretation engine based on the meanings, symbols and relationships of the **78 tarot cards**, comprising the 22 Major Arcana and the 56 Minor Arcana.

Each card has associated concepts and meanings that the system analyses using a set of predefined rules. In spreads with several cards, the engine considers the selected cards, their positions and the relationships between their meanings to build a coherent interpretation.

**ArcanaLogic does not use generative artificial intelligence to create answers for tarot readings.** Readings are produced using ArcanaLogic’s own algorithm, which analyses the meanings of the cards and the relationships between them in keeping with traditional tarot principles and symbolism.

This does not mean that the system knows your future or can determine what will happen. Interpretations offer symbolic perspectives to help you reflect on your circumstances and decisions.

## 2. Are the cards in a reading selected at random?

Selecting the cards is part of each reading. You choose from the cards available on screen, and ArcanaLogic interprets the cards selected.

A card’s meaning does not change arbitrarily to fit an answer. It is based on the concepts and rules established for the interpretation engine.

Two readings with different cards can therefore produce different interpretations, with the cards and their relationships forming the basis of each result.

## 3. What is the difference between a three-card reading and a Yes/No consultation?

A **three-card reading** allows you to explore a situation from different perspectives. The engine analyses the meaning and position of each card and the relationship between the three to produce an interpretation of the spread as a whole.

A **Yes/No consultation** is designed to offer more direct guidance on a specific question.

In this format, ArcanaLogic uses a single card and evaluates its meanings using specific rules to determine a yes or no answer.

The answer is not determined solely by treating a card as good or bad. The concepts associated with the card, its nature and the established interpretation criteria also play a role.

Both formats are for guidance and offer no guarantees about future events.

## 4. Can ArcanaLogic predict my future?

No. ArcanaLogic cannot know or guarantee future events.

Tarot is a tool for symbolic interpretation that can be used to reflect on experiences, possibilities, emotions and decisions.

ArcanaLogic’s answers should be understood as entertainment and personal guidance, not as infallible predictions.

They should not replace advice from qualified professionals on medical, psychological, legal, financial or other matters requiring specialist knowledge.

## 7. Can I save and view my earlier readings?

ArcanaLogic keeps completed readings and their results linked to your account.

Whether you can view your history in the application depends on the enabled features and the plan available to you.

Storing a reading and being able to access it in your history are separate matters: a reading may remain linked to the account even if access through the interface is limited.

If you delete your account, your history and associated personal data will be included in the deletion process, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

## 8. Do I need to create an account to use ArcanaLogic?

ArcanaLogic lets you start with an **anonymous account** managed through Firebase Authentication, without initially providing an email address or password.

ArcanaLogic also provides for linking your account to Google and, when available, to Apple.

Linking associates the provider’s identity with your existing account and preserves your history and related data when the process is completed successfully.

It may be harder to recover access to an anonymous account if you lose your session, change devices or uninstall the application.

We therefore recommend linking your account to an available authentication provider if you want to make recovery easier.

## 10. How can I delete my ArcanaLogic account?

ArcanaLogic provides for a procedure to request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it.

When this feature is available, you will be able to make the request directly in the application:

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Select **Delete my account**.
3. Review the information about the consequences of deletion.
4. Explicitly confirm your request.

The application will record your request and notify you that it has been received.

**The planned maximum time to complete deletion is 48 hours from receipt of the request.**

Once the procedure is complete, you will receive confirmation through an available and verified channel.

If you cannot access the application, you will be able to request deletion by writing to:

**{{accountDeletionEmail}}**

For security reasons, it will be necessary to verify sufficiently that you own the account before deleting it.

Deletion will cover the account, profile, reading requests and history, and other associated personal data, except where data must be kept to comply with legal obligations.

Data in backups may remain temporarily until it is removed in accordance with the relevant rotation cycle.

**Important:** deleting your ArcanaLogic account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or Apple App Store subscription. You will need to manage cancellation through the relevant store.

The deletion procedure from Profile is planned for future implementation and should not be considered operational until it is enabled.
