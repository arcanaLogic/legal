## 2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ArcanaLogic collects, uses, stores, protects and deletes its users’ personal data.

It applies to use of the mobile application, account creation and management, tarot readings, Yes/No consultations, horoscopes, subscription services, advertising and communications relating to the platform.

It also covers requests received through official contact channels and the information pages published by ArcanaLogic.

Some features may be under development or available only in certain versions of the application. Processing associated with those features will begin when they are actually enabled.

Personal data will be processed in accordance with the purposes and legal bases described in this policy. Where consent is needed, it will be requested specifically.

## 3. Account, authentication and profile

### 3.1. Account creation and access

ArcanaLogic uses Firebase Authentication to manage user identification and authentication.

The application provides for the following access methods:

- **Anonymous account:** allows users to use available features without initially providing a name or email address.
- **Google account:** allows users to authenticate using a Google account.
- **Apple account:** an authentication method planned for compatible versions in which it is enabled.

Each account has a unique identifier provided by Firebase and an internal identifier that ArcanaLogic uses to associate account data with its operations.

When a user uses an external authentication provider, that provider may disclose identity data needed to provide the service to ArcanaLogic, depending on the permissions granted and the information available.

The application may automatically generate a username if one has not been provided by the user or the authentication provider.

### 3.2. Profile information

Users may voluntarily provide additional information to personalise their experience.

This information may include:

- Name or user identifier.
- Pronouns.
- Date and time of birth.
- City and country of birth.
- City and country of residence.
- Degree of belief in or affinity with tarot.
- Prior experience with tarot.
- Preferred interpretation style.
- Application language.

Optional fields may be left blank. Leaving them blank does not necessarily prevent access to basic features.

The date of birth may be used to determine the zodiac sign and provide horoscope-related content.

Users may request rectification of their profile data and, where the relevant controls are available in the application, may change it directly there.

## 4. Readings, history and horoscopes

### 4.1. Tarot readings

To provide a tarot reading, ArcanaLogic processes the information needed to generate, display and retain the result.

This information may include the cards selected, their positions in the spread, the language, certain profile data and the results of earlier readings used to put the interpretation in context.

Requests and results are linked to the account by internal identifiers.

The history allows users to retrieve earlier readings where the features and their subscribed plan permit it.

### 4.2. Yes/No consultations

In Yes/No consultations, ArcanaLogic may process the card selected and the question entered by the user.

The question may be stored with the result so it can be viewed later and the history remains consistent.

**Users must avoid entering particularly sensitive information or third parties’ personal data** in questions or consultations.

Questions may contain personal information freely provided by the user. ArcanaLogic will use this content to provide the requested feature and will not use it for purposes outside those described in this policy.

### 4.3. Personalisation of interpretations

Interpretations may use certain profile data and previous results to tailor the content to the user’s experience.

Where necessary, the service may retain a copy of the data used for a particular request. A later change to the profile does not necessarily alter the content of readings already carried out.

Interpretation workflows run on infrastructure hosted in the European Union.

The data provided for these operations will be limited to what is needed to provide the relevant feature.

### 4.4. Horoscopes

Horoscopes use the zodiac sign and language to select and display content.

The sign may be determined from the date of birth provided by the user.

Generating general content for zodiac signs does not require disclosing the user’s identity or date of birth to the content provider.

Access to certain horoscope formats may depend on the plan available.

## 5. Data stored on the device

ArcanaLogic may store information locally on the device to retain user preferences and support the operation of the application.

This data includes:

- Selected language.
- Initial setup and welcome status.
- Version of the Terms accepted.
- Display preferences for certain elements.
- Temporary information needed to recover pending operations.
- Identifiers linked to ongoing reading requests.

For Yes/No consultations, temporary information may include the question entered by the user while the operation remains pending.

This data is used to maintain service continuity and prevent unnecessary loss of information during operations.

Removing the application may remove locally stored data, depending on the operating system and its backup or restore mechanisms.

**Uninstalling ArcanaLogic does not automatically delete the account or data stored on the servers.**

## 6. Plans, usage limits and purchases

ArcanaLogic offers free and paid access options with different features and usage limits.

To manage plans and usage limits, the platform may process:

- Internal user identifier.
- Assigned plan.
- Subscription status.
- Start, renewal, change and end dates.
- Usage limits and recorded usage.
- History of operations relating to access entitlements.

When purchases through Google Play or Apple App Store are enabled, ArcanaLogic may receive and retain transaction identifiers, products, purchase statuses and other technical information needed to verify subscriptions and prevent fraudulent use.

Payments are handled by the relevant distribution platforms.

ArcanaLogic does not need to receive or store the full payment card numbers used to make purchases on those platforms.

Payment providers and application stores apply their own privacy policies to the data they process directly.

Transaction information may be kept for the periods required by applicable legal obligations, including after account deletion, where applicable.

## 7. Advertising and preferences

ArcanaLogic may display advertisements through Appodeal and the advertising networks integrated into its services.

Advertising may include banners and rewarded ads that provide access to certain features.

Advertising providers may process technical information such as:

- IP address.
- Device advertising identifiers, where available and where their use is permitted.
- Technical characteristics of the device and operating system.
- Information about ad views and interactions.
- Privacy and consent preferences.
- Technical data needed to prevent advertising fraud.

### 7.1. Personalised advertising

**Personalised advertising will be used only where the relevant consent has been given and is valid under applicable rules.**

ArcanaLogic uses consent management mechanisms to inform users and collect their preferences where necessary.

Acceptance of the Terms of Use does not constitute consent to receive personalised advertising.

Users may refuse or withdraw consent for processing that depends on it through the privacy mechanisms available in the application and, where applicable, the operating system controls.

Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 7.2. Non-personalised advertising

Where there is no consent for personalised advertising, non-personalised ads may be displayed if the processing and the technologies used have a valid legal basis.

Non-personalised advertising may also involve limited processing of technical information needed to display ads, prevent fraud or carry out permitted operations.

### 7.3. Rewarded ads

Voluntarily viewing a rewarded ad may be used to unlock an in-app benefit.

Choosing to view that ad does not in itself constitute consent to process data for ad personalisation.

## 8. Purposes and legal bases

ArcanaLogic processes personal data for the following purposes, in accordance with the applicable legal bases.

### 8.1. Provision of the service

Account creation, authentication, management of necessary profiles, readings, consultations, horoscopes, history and plan management are based, where necessary to provide the requested service, on **performance of a contract** under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.

### 8.2. Voluntary personalisation

Optional profile data is used to provide the personalisation features requested by the user.

Where processing is unnecessary to provide the service and requires consent, that consent will be requested specifically.

If a feature involves processing special categories of personal data, a valid condition under Article 9 of the GDPR must also be met before that processing takes place.

### 8.3. Security and prevention of abuse

Account protection, request verification, fraud prevention, access control and technical diagnostics may be based on **legitimate interests** under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR where the processing is necessary and proportionate and the users’ rights do not override those interests.

### 8.4. Personalised advertising

Advertising-related processing that requires consent will be based on the **user’s consent** under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, without prejudice to additional requirements applicable to access to or storage of information on devices.

### 8.5. Compliance with legal obligations

Processing necessary to meet tax, administrative or data protection obligations or other legally enforceable requirements will be based on **compliance with legal obligations** under Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR.

### 8.6. Handling enquiries and requests

Data provided when contacting ArcanaLogic will be used to handle and respond to the relevant enquiry or request.

The legal basis will depend on its nature and may include provision of the service, compliance with legal obligations or legitimate interests where applicable.

## 11. Data retention

### 11.1. Account and profile data

Personal data associated with the account will be kept **for as long as the account remains active and until it is deleted**, unless a legal obligation requires certain data to be kept for an additional period.

This rule covers profile data and identifiers associated with the account.

### 11.2. Readings and history

Requests, questions, interpretations and results associated with the history will be kept while the account exists, to allow users to consult the history and use related features.

When the account is deleted, this data will be included in the erasure process, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

### 11.3. Plans, usage limits and commercial operations

Plan and usage limit records will be kept for the lifetime of the account where needed to manage access entitlements.

Supporting documents, transactions and other records subject to tax, accounting or legal obligations may be kept for the periods required by law, with access restricted to those purposes.

### 11.4. Temporary and technical data

Data needed for pending operations will be kept for as long as it is needed to complete, recover or manage those operations.

Technical and security logs will be kept for periods necessary and proportionate to their purposes, in accordance with the operating rules of the relevant services.

### 11.5. Backups

ArcanaLogic may keep backups to ensure the integrity and recovery of its services.

**Data deleted from active systems may remain temporarily in backups until it is removed in accordance with the established rotation cycle.**

Backups will be used for recovery and service continuity and will not be used to deliberately restore deleted accounts.

If an earlier backup is used to recover the service, the applicable deletions must be reapplied.

### 11.6. Account deletion

Once account deletion is complete, its personal data will be erased from active systems, except for data that must be kept to comply with a legal obligation.

Temporary retention in backups and retention required by law do not mean that the account remains active.

## 12. Security

ArcanaLogic applies technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or improper disclosure.

The mechanisms used include:

- Authentication through Firebase Authentication.
- Identity verification using authentication tokens.
- Application integrity checks using Firebase App Check and compatible mechanisms.
- HTTPS communications with certificate validation in the relevant integrations.
- Linking operations to verified account identifiers.
- Access controls for profile and reading information.
- Mechanisms to prevent duplicate requests and unauthorised operations.
- Limited processing of sensitive information in certain error logs.

These measures will be reviewed and adapted as the platform develops and identified risks change.

No computer system can guarantee absolute security. ArcanaLogic will seek to apply measures appropriate to the risks and handle security incidents in accordance with its legal obligations.

## 14. Account deletion

ArcanaLogic envisages an account deletion procedure to allow users to request erasure of their account and associated personal data.

### 14.1. Request from the application

The planned procedure will allow users to:

1. Open the **Profile** screen.
2. Select **Delete my account**.
3. Review the consequences of deletion.
4. Explicitly confirm the request.
5. Receive confirmation that the request has been recorded.

The request will be linked to the authenticated account.

### 14.2. Deletion timeframe

**The planned operational commitment is to complete account deletion within a maximum of 48 hours from receipt of the request.**

This timeframe applies to erasure of the account and its personal data from active systems, subject to applicable legal retention exceptions.

It does not necessarily mean that data in backups subject to rotation cycles will disappear immediately.

Once the procedure is complete, confirmation will be sent to the user through an available and verified channel.

### 14.3. Requests by email

Users who cannot access the application may use the alternative channel:

**{{accountDeletionEmail}}**

To protect the account against fraudulent requests, ArcanaLogic may require sufficient verification of account ownership before carrying out the deletion.

For requests received through this channel, the operational deletion period will begin once the request has been sufficiently identified and verified.

### 14.4. Scope of deletion

The procedure will cover, as applicable:

- The account identity managed through Firebase Authentication.
- Profile and associated personal data.
- Reading requests and history.
- Questions and results from Yes/No consultations.
- Usage and usage limit records linked to the account that are not subject to a legal retention requirement.
- Other personal data associated with the account that is not subject to a valid retention obligation.

Data that must be kept to comply with legal obligations will be retained solely for those purposes and for the required period.

### 14.5. Active subscriptions

**Deleting an ArcanaLogic account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed through Google Play or Apple App Store.**

Users must manage cancellation through the relevant store to prevent later renewals, where applicable.

### 14.6. Implementation status

The procedure for requesting deletion from Profile, recording the request and carrying out erasure are features planned for a future implementation.

Until the procedure has been enabled and verified, it must not be presented in the application or published documentation as an operational service.
