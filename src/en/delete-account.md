# ACCOUNT DELETION — ARCANALOGIC

**Candidate version:** 1.0  
**Draft date:** 2 November 2026  
**Status:** Pending legal and technical review and approval for publication.

## 1. How can I delete my ArcanaLogic account?

At ArcanaLogic, we respect your decision to stop using our services and want to make it easy for you to request account deletion in a secure and transparent way.

You can request deletion of your account and its associated personal data from the application or through the email address provided for that purpose.

**This page is public and you can view it without signing in to ArcanaLogic.**

## 2. Request from the application

When the feature is enabled, you will be able to request deletion directly from ArcanaLogic by following these steps:

1. Open ArcanaLogic and sign in to your account if necessary.
2. Go to the **Profile** section.
3. Select **Delete my account**.
4. Review the information about the consequences of deletion.
5. Explicitly confirm that you want to delete your account.

Once you confirm the request, the application will send it as an authenticated request to our services for recording and processing.

Account deletion is irreversible. Once complete, you will be unable to recover the profile, readings or history associated with that account.

## 3. Request without access to the application

If you have lost access to your account, changed devices or cannot use the application, you will also be able to request deletion by email.

**Email address for account deletion requests:**

**{{accountDeletionEmail}}**

To help us handle your request, we recommend stating that you wish to delete your ArcanaLogic account and providing the information needed to identify it.

For security reasons, we may ask for additional information to verify sufficiently that you own the account.

This check is intended to prevent an unauthorised person from requesting deletion of someone else’s account.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes or other unnecessary confidential information.

## 4. How long does deletion take?

**ArcanaLogic sets a maximum of 48 hours to complete account deletion from receipt of a valid request.**

If you request deletion from the application, the period will start when our services successfully record the authenticated request.

If you request deletion by email, the period will start once we have received the necessary information and sufficiently verified ownership of the account.

Once the procedure is complete, we will send confirmation through an available and verified communication channel.

This period applies to deletion of the account and its personal data from active systems, subject to the legal exceptions and backups described below.

## 5. What data is deleted?

Deletion covers personal data associated with your ArcanaLogic account, including:

- **User account:** identity and records associated with authentication through Firebase Authentication.
- **Personal profile:** username, birth details, residence and personalisation preferences, where provided.
- **Tarot readings:** requests, selected cards, results and related history.
- **Yes/No consultations:** stored questions, cards and answers.
- **Usage data:** account-linked records of reading usage and allowances that are not legally required to be kept.
- **Other associated data:** personal information linked to the account that is no longer needed and is not subject to a valid retention obligation.

Deletion also applies to anonymous accounts, provided that their ownership can be sufficiently verified.

Once deletion is complete, you will no longer have access to the deleted account’s services or history.

## 6. Is any data kept after my account is deleted?

As a general rule, personal data associated with the account is kept while the account exists and deleted when the erasure process is complete.

However, there are two situations that may require limited retention.

### 6.1. Legal obligations

Certain records, especially those relating to commercial transactions, tax obligations or legally enforceable requirements, may be retained for the periods established by applicable law.

This data will be used solely for the purposes that justify keeping it.

### 6.2. Backups

Data deleted from active systems may remain temporarily in backups intended to support service recovery and continuity.

**The information will be removed from these backups according to their rotation cycle.**

Backups will not be used to deliberately reactivate deleted accounts.

If an earlier backup needs to be restored, the relevant deletions must be applied again.

## 7. What happens to my subscription?

**Deleting your ArcanaLogic account does not automatically cancel an active subscription.**

Subscriptions are managed through the application store where they were purchased.

If you have a Google Play subscription, you will need to cancel it through the subscription options in your Google Play account.

If you use a version of ArcanaLogic with subscriptions through the Apple App Store, you will need to manage them through the subscription settings in your Apple account.

We recommend cancelling any active subscription before requesting account deletion to avoid later renewals.

Deleting the account does not automatically entitle you to a refund of amounts paid. Refund requests are governed by the relevant store’s terms and procedures and applicable law.

## 8. Important information before you continue

Before requesting deletion of your account, please bear in mind that:

- Deletion is irreversible once complete.
- You will lose access to your profile and the associated reading history.
- Deleted consultations and results cannot be recovered.
- If you wish to use ArcanaLogic again, you will need to create a new account.
- Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through the relevant store.
- Some data may be retained temporarily in backups or for periods required by law.
